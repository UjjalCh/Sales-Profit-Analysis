📊 Sales and Profit Analysis Dashboard
📌 Project Overview
Developed an interactive Sales and Profit Analysis Dashboard to analyze business performance and sales trends.
The dashboard provides insights into total sales, profit, orders, customers, products, and regional performance.
Designed to help businesses make data-driven decisions through interactive visualizations and filters.

🎯 Project Objectives
Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
Identify the best-performing states, regions, and product categories.
Understand sales trends across different years, quarters, and months.
Evaluate customer segments and payment methods.
Identify top-selling products and profitable sub-categories.
Explore the relationship between sales and profit.

📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
Total Sales: 2.30M
Total Profit: 286.40K
Total Orders: 5K
Average Discount: 0.16
Total Customers: 793
Total Products: 2K

📊 Dashboard Features
States by Sales: Compares sales performance across different states.
Sales by Order Day: Analyzes sales across different days.
Sales by Segment: Displays sales distribution among Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office segments.
Sales by State and Category: Visualizes geographical sales performance by product category.
Profit by Category: Shows the contribution of Technology, Office Supplies, and Furniture to total profit.
Profit by Sub-Category: Compares profitability across product sub-categories.
Top 5 Sold Products: Highlights the five best-selling products.
Sales vs Profit: Shows the relationship between sales and profit.
Profit by Region: Compares profits across West, East, Central, and South regions.
Sales and Profit by Year: Tracks annual sales and profit trends.
Total Profit by Sub-Category: Displays profit contributions across sub-categories.
Sales by Quarter and Month: Analyzes monthly sales trends and quarterly performance.
